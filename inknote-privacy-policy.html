InkNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026
App: InkNote for Android
Operator: The Killer

Who this policy covers

This policy describes InkNote for Android, version 1.3 (version code 3). InkNote is intended for people of all ages, including children. We will update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change.

Notes and information stored on your device

InkNote does not require an account or sign-in and does not operate its own cloud-sync service. In version 1.3, your note titles and text, sketches, profile choices (such as a nickname), and app preferences are stored in app-private storage on your Android device. InkNote does not send this content to an InkNote-operated server. Version 1.3 does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Android backup and device transfer

InkNote 1.3 allows Android backup. If backup is enabled on your device, Android Auto Backup may copy app-private files—including notes, sketches, and preferences—to your Google Drive backup. Android may also transfer app data directly to another device during device setup, depending on Android version, device maker, and transfer settings. These are Android system backup or transfer features, not an InkNote account or InkNote-operated cloud sync. A backup may be restored after reinstalling the app or setting up another device. Check your Android backup and transfer settings if you do not want this to happen.

Optional microphone feature

If you tap Talk and allow microphone access, InkNote records a short voice clip. Inky repeats your words after you pause, with a voice effect. In version 1.3, the clip is processed on your device for playback and is not sent to The Killer or an InkNote server. The temporary clip is kept in the app cache, which Android Auto Backup excludes by default, and is normally deleted after playback, when playback is stopped, or when you leave the feature. If the app is interrupted, a temporary cache file may remain until the app or Android clears it. You can deny microphone access and still use the note-taking features.

Please use the microphone feature only with appropriate permission from anyone whose voice may be recorded. Parents or guardians should supervise a child’s microphone use where appropriate.

Sharing and export

If you choose to export a note or share it through Android’s share interface, the destination app you select receives the content you chose to send. That app handles it under its own privacy terms. The Killer does not receive a copy through InkNote’s share action.

Ads and other services

Version 1.3 does not display ads and does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, login service, or InkNote-operated cloud service. If a future version adds ads or another service that changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures before releasing that version.

Retention and deletion

Your notes, sketches, profile choices, and preferences remain in local app storage until you delete them. Notes in InkNote’s recycle bin remain there until you restore or permanently delete them. Uninstalling InkNote normally removes its local app data, but Android backup or device-transfer features may restore data from a backup. Temporary voice clips are intended to be removed after use; an interrupted session may leave a cache file until cleanup. InkNote has no user accounts or account-deletion service.

Children

InkNote is intended for children as well as older users. Version 1.3 does not require sign-in and does not send notes, sketches, or voice clips to an InkNote-operated server. Android backup may handle app-private notes and settings as explained above. The optional voice feature starts only when a user taps Talk and grants Android microphone permission. Parents and guardians can help children choose what to save or share and supervise microphone use.

Changes to this policy

If InkNote’s features or data practices change, The Killer will revise this policy and update the date above. The current published version will be available at a stable public web address and from within InkNote.
